Hebrew Calendar is for people who live by the Hebrew date. Set a Hebrew birthday, a yahrzeit or an anniversary once, and it shows up on the right day every year. No calculations, no searching a printed calendar.

The app is simple. The Hebrew date is the main axis of the calendar, not a small add-on in the corner. Holidays and the weekly Torah portion are already there, and everything syncs both ways with Google Calendar.

A calendar built on the Hebrew date
• Month, week, day and agenda views, with the Hebrew and Gregorian dates side by side
• Holidays, fasts, Rosh Chodesh, Israeli national days and the weekly parsha, for Israel or the diaspora
• Jump to any Hebrew or Gregorian date, and search all your events by word
• Switch the calendar to Gregorian months with a tap on the title
• A line marking the current time in the week and day views
• Choose the text size, from 90% to 140%, and touch a day in the month view to enlarge it

Times of day and Shabbat
• Dawn, sunrise, latest Shema and prayer, midday, mincha, sunset and nightfall
• Candle lighting and Havdalah by the city's custom, by city or by the phone's location

Daily learning
• On the first visit each day: the day's Kitzur Shulchan Aruch reading, in a cycle that finishes the book every year
• The weekly Torah portion, an aliyah a day: Shnayim Mikra with Onkelos, with Rashi, or Chumash with Rashi
• Choose in settings, under "Daily learning"

Events that repeat by the Hebrew date
• Hebrew birthdays, anniversaries, yahrzeits, or any event on the same Hebrew date
• Yearly, monthly, weekly or daily recurrence, by the Hebrew or the Gregorian date
• "Gregorian to Hebrew": enter a Gregorian birth, death or wedding date and the app works out the Hebrew date, before or after sunset
• Correct handling of leap years (Adar I and Adar II) and 29-day months
• Edit or delete one occurrence, this and following, or the whole series

Meetings, colors and participants
• A color for every event, using Google Calendar's colors
• Invite participants by email. Google sends the invitation and you can see who accepted
• Phone numbers, emails and links in an event are tappable: call, WhatsApp, mail, or open a Zoom link
• Open the location in Google Maps or Waze

Reminders
• Phone notifications before an event, at the time you choose

Google Calendar sync
• Two-way sync: events created here appear in Google, and vice versa
• Pull the screen down to sync right away

Backup and restore
• Export all your events to a file, send it to yourself, and restore at any time

Privacy
• Your data is stored in your own account only, and other users cannot access it

Hebrew Calendar is part of the KALFAPP family, a volunteer project of small, simple apps for everyday life. Have an idea for an improvement? We'd love to hear it.
