Hebrew Calendar is a full calendar app where the Hebrew (Jewish) date is the main axis, not a small add-on in the corner.

📅 Hebrew-date views
• Month, week, day and agenda views showing the Hebrew and Gregorian dates side by side
• Full Hebrew, right-to-left interface
• A "Today" button to jump back to the current date

🔁 Events that repeat by the Hebrew date
• Hebrew birthdays, anniversaries, yahrzeits, or any event that recurs every year on the same Hebrew date
• Monthly recurrence by the Hebrew date, plus weekly and daily recurrence
• Correct handling of leap years (Adar I and Adar II) and 29-day months
• Set an end date for a series, or delete a single occurrence

🔔 Reminders
• Phone notifications before an event, at the time you choose

🔄 Google Calendar sync
• Two-way sync: events created here appear in Google Calendar, and vice versa
• Secure sign-in with your Google account

💾 Backup and restore
• Export all your events to a file and restore them at any time

🔒 Privacy
• Your data is stored in your own account only; other users cannot access it
